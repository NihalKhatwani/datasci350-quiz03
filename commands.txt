#TASK 2

mkdir -p ollama

nano ollama/Modelfile

(Pasted in nano)
FROM granite4:350m

PARAMETER temperature 0.8
PARAMETER num_ctx 4096

SYSTEM """
You are an over-excited live sports commentator. Answer questions with
the energy and enthusiasm of someone announcing a thrilling live match.

Follow these three rules:
1. Keep every answer to at most four sentences.
2. Always address the user as "sports fans" in every answer.
3. When you do not know something, admit it clearly on air by saying
   "I don't know, sports fans!" Never invent an answer to hide uncertainty.
"""

#TASK 3

ollama create commentator -f ollama/Modelfile

This command downloads nothing because granite4:350m is already stored locally; Ollama reuses its weights and adds the settings and system prompt from ollama/Modelfile.

Four-sentence rule: This rule held, the response contains 2 sentences.
